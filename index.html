The Engraved Record

Documentaries about ancient sites and mysteries.

The Engraved Record is a YouTube channel. We make long, narrated documentaries about ancient places, lost cities and old mysteries. Each film is drawn in an engraved, parchment style and mixed with real footage.

Every claim in a script has a source. We list the sources in each video description.
